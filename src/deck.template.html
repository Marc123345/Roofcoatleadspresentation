What to expect

20 to 50 inquiries a month per market.

Inquiries, not jobs

An inquiry is a property owner asking for a quote. How many become jobs depends on your follow-up, your pricing and your close rate.

Every market is different

Roof stock, season and local competition all move the number. Some markets land near the top of the range and some near the bottom.
