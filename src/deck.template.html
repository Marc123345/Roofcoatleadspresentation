What to expect

20 to 50 inquiries a month per market.

01

Inquiries, not jobs

An inquiry is a property owner asking for a quote. How many become jobs depends on your follow-up, your pricing and your close rate.

02

Every market is different

Roof stock, season and local competition all move the number. Some markets land near the top of the range and some near the bottom.

03

Sales and marketing

Every lead gen company has the same problem: marketing can’t see what sales does.

01

The usual gap

The agency sends leads and the contractor works them, but neither side sees the other’s half. The agency guesses which leads were good. The contractor decides the leads are bad.

02

What closes it

Every lead’s stage and notes in LeadConnector. We can see which inquiries were quoted, which went quiet, and why.

03

What we do with it

Each week we go through the pipeline with you and change the ads, the form or the targeting based on what your notes show.

A lead with no notes is a lead we can’t learn from. The contractors who get the most from us are the ones who keep the pipeline current.

After you sign

How we work together.

From us

  • A weekly call to go through your pipeline
  • We read your LeadConnector notes before each call

Your pipeline and notes are how we see which inquiries turn into jobs. They are what the weekly call runs on.
